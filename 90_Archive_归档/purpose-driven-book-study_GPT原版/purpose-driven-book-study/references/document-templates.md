## Minimum quality bar

A stage document is incomplete if it only summarizes what the source says. It must also contain:

- at least one explicit validity boundary;
- at least one connection to the active workstream;
- at least one verification or falsification method;
- unresolved questions when evidence is insufficient;
- exact source locations when page or section information is available.
